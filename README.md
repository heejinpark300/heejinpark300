# Heejin Park, Ph.D.

### AI in Education | Human–AI Interaction | Educational Technology

I am an educational researcher with a Ph.D. in English Education, exploring how artificial intelligence can support human learning, teaching, and decision-making.

My research interests include **AI in Education, Responsible AI, AI Literacy, Human–AI Collaboration, and Technology-Enhanced Language Learning**.

I develop educational AI prototypes to connect pedagogical research with practical applications of machine learning, deep learning, and web-based technologies.

### 02. AI Feedback Acceptance Predictor

**Individual Project | Machine Learning**

A machine learning prototype that explores the classification of simulated learner responses to AI-generated feedback: Accept, Edit, or Reject.

**Technologies:** Python, scikit-learn, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Streamlit

**Dataset:** 300 synthetically generated observations.

[View Source Code](https://github.com/heejinpark300/AI-Feedback-Acceptance-Predictor) | [Live Demo](https://ai-feedback-acceptance-predictor.streamlit.app)

### 03. AI Learning Engagement Classifier

**Individual Project | Deep Learning & Computer Vision**

A MobileNetV2-based prototype for classifying learning-scene images into dataset-defined Engagement and Disengagement categories.

**Technologies:** Python, TensorFlow, Keras, MobileNetV2, Streamlit

**Test Accuracy:** 69.53% on 1,116 held-out images.

[View Source Code](https://github.com/heejinpark300/AI-Learning-Engagement-Classifier) | [Live Demo](https://ai-learning-engagement-classifier-2ednmkmvfk972jz2qq22fr.streamlit.app)
